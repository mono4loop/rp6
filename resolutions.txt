Desktop screens (panel @ desktop scale). Every desktop variant must fit each one:
the window variant at the windowed size (850x950, shorter on short screens), the
console maximized (the screen less ~69 for GNOME's top bar and title bar) and
full screen. cmd/rp6/screens_test.go (TestContentFitsScreens) checks the content
minimums against these budgets; keep the two lists in step.
* 1920x1080@1 - full HD
* 1920x1080@1.25 - full HD laptop at 125%
* 1920x1200@1 - Thinkpad X13, 100%
* 1920x1200@1.25 - Thinkpad X13 at 125% (1536x960 logical; maximized 1536x891)
* 2256x1504@1.5 - Framework 13
* 2560x1440@1 - QHD
* 2560x1440@1.25 - QHD at 125%
* 2560x1600@2 - WQXGA laptop at 200% (1280x800 logical, the smallest supported)
* 2880x1800@2 - Retina-class laptop at 200%
* 3440x1440@1 - Asus ROG 34" ultrawide (21:9)
* 3840x2160@1.5 - 4K at 150%
* 3840x2160@2 - 4K at 200%
* 1366x768@1 - unsupported: neither variant fits (the console needs ~1600 logical width, the window ~800 height)

The gate can't see what the compositor decides: libdecor's title-bar
double-click, Mutter's maximize constraints, real panel heights (KDE, docks) or
multi-monitor placement. Check those by hand: double-click the title bar for the
console, again for the window.
